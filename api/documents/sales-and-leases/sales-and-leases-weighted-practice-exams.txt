# Sales and Leases Weighted Practice Exams

Ten practice exams (150 new questions) for the Week 10 assessment, with each topic's share of questions set by how testable it is. Tier 1 topics, the bright-line rules most likely to be tested, get 66 questions: risk of loss and identification, implied warranties, express warranties, title and entrustment, and warranty disclaimers. Tier 2 topics get 64: tender, inspection, acceptance and payment, course of performance and waiver, modification, quantity and price, the parol evidence rule, assignment and delegation, insecurity and repudiation, excuse, and remedy limits. Tier 3 topics get 20: scope, formation and § 2-207, the statute of frauds, good faith and variation by agreement, and termination. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format: 15 stand-alone, five-choice questions that mix short hypos asking who wins and why with rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…"). Wrong answers are built from the near-miss rules she tests, such as merchant vs. non-merchant, tender vs. receipt, void vs. voidable title, and "as is" vs. specific language. Some questions have a "None of the above" choice, and sometimes it is the answer, so those questions keep their letters. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam (the real one is 30 minutes) and then grade it.
