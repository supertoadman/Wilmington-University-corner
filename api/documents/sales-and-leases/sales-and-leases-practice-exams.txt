58 cumulative practice exams (870 questions) for the final, covering all of Weeks 1–14 and all 35 sections of the course outline, from which law governs and the battle of the forms through warranties, risk of loss, buyer's and seller's remedies, privity, and leases under Article 2A. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format: 15 stand-alone, five-choice questions that mix rule questions (“Which of the following is NOT…”) with short hypos asking who wins and why, plus “None of the above” and “All of the above” choices that are sometimes the answer. Every exam samples the whole course, and about 40% of the questions come from the Weeks 8–14 remedies and lease units, including integrated hypos and damages calculations. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check answers as you go, or Exam mode to grade a full exam, and pick a topic to drill one outline section by week.

Every exam samples the whole course, and 334 questions come from the remedies and lease units in Weeks 8–14. Every choice has its own explanation.
