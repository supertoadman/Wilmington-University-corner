# Sales and Leases Acronym Exams

Four practice exams (60 new questions) on just the acronyms: the shipping and delivery terms and the course shorthand. 40 questions cover the delivery terms in §§ 2-319 to 2-324 and how they interact with risk of loss: F.O.B. place of shipment and place of destination, F.O.B. vessel and F.A.S., C.I.F. and C. & F., ex-ship, “no arrival, no sale,” C.O.D. and payment against documents, and what happens when there is no term or one side is in breach. The other 20 test the shorthand from the outline: SOF, PER, NOM, CoP/CoD/UoT, LD, SOL, GFP and BIOCB, HTA and LOC (with PDM), DTW and CORS (Mathis), and UETA signatures. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format: 15 stand-alone, five-choice questions that mix short hypos asking who bears the loss and why with rule questions (“Which of the following is NOT…”). Wrong answers are built from the near-miss rules: F.A.S. vs. F.O.B. vessel, paying freight vs. bearing the risk, payment vs. acceptance, void vs. voidable title. Some questions have a “None of the above” choice. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it.
