# Sales and Leases Focused Practice Exams

Four practice exams (60 new questions) on four topics only: assignment and delegation, liquidated damages, warranties, and limited remedies. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format: 15 stand-alone questions with five choices each, mixing short hypos that ask who wins and why with rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…"). Every exam covers all four topics. Warranties get the most weight: express and implied warranties, title and infringement, disclaimers, cumulation, and privity under § 2-318. No fact pattern repeats the other practice sets, and questions built on the casebook's cases (Rogath, Razor, Goodin) give you new facts that track the case instead of asking what happened in it. Some choices are "None of the above" or "All of the above", and sometimes that is the answer, so those choices stay in exam order. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam (the real one is 30 minutes) and then grade it.
