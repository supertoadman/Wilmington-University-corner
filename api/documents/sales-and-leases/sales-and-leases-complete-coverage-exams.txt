# Sales and Leases Complete Coverage Exams

31 quick-fire practice exams (465 new questions) that together test every rule and concept from Weeks 1–8: each statute section and Official Comment the course relies on, every slide, every main, note and cited case, and every assigned problem. A checklist of 446 rules and concepts, built from the slides, the Sepinuck casebook and the statutory supplement, was used to write them, and every item on it is tested at least once. The questions are short so you can move fast: most are one- or two-line rule questions or mini-hypos, and many “Which of the following is NOT…” questions test four or five rules at once. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format (15 five-choice questions) and samples the whole course, from which law governs and the battle of the forms through warranties, risk of loss, disclaimers, remedies, the parol evidence rule, assignment, repudiation and excuse. Wrong answers are built from the near misses: the common-law rule the UCC changed, the wrong number or section, merchant vs. non-merchant, and the rule from the next section over. Some questions offer “None of the above” or “All of the above,” and sometimes that is the answer. 23 questions change the facts of another question (“Same contract, but…”); each one names the question it builds on and shows its facts, so a shuffle, a topic filter or a different exam never leaves you without them. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it; pick a topic to drill one area.
