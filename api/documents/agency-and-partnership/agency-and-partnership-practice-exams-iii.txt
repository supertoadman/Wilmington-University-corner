Agency & Partnership Practice Exams III
=======================================

Subject: Agency & Partnership
Type: Practice Questions
Source: https://supertoadman.github.io/Wilmington-University-corner/subjects/agency-and-partnership/agency-and-partnership-practice-exams-iii/

A third set of 14 practice exams (210 new questions) for the Week 10 exam, in the same format as the first two: Professor Richardson's sample-exam style, with 15 five-choice questions per exam, six stand-alone questions followed by two shared hypos (Q7–11 and Q12–15), and combination choices such as "B and C", "A, B and C", and "None of the above." All 28 hypos are new fact patterns, among them a vineyard partner's expulsion, a nephew selling his aunt's car, a solar lender that runs its borrower, a nightclub bouncer, a UPA laundromat freeze-out, a fired club manager's lingering authority, a coffee franchisor's espresso machines, an LLC formed after its lease was signed, a town's fireworks contractor, a festival lot's taken lease, a surf shop's winding up, and a lender to a cidery. Together they cover all 14 sections of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, and every choice has its own explanation. Study mode checks each answer as you pick it; Exam mode lets you answer a full exam and then grade it. Filter by exam or outline section, shuffle the questions, and retry the ones you missed.

# Agency & Partnership Practice Exams III

A third set of 14 practice exams (210 new questions) for the Week 10 Agency & Partnership exam, in the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 questions with five choices each. Questions 1–6 stand alone. Questions 7–11 and 12–15 share a hypo. Some choices combine others ("B and C", "A, B and C", "None of the above"), so the choices stay in exam order. Every hypo is new, none repeats a fact pattern from the first two sets, and together the exams again cover every section of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it.
