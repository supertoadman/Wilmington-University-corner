14 practice exams (210 questions) for the Week 10 exam, written in the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 five-choice questions each, with six stand-alone questions followed by two shared hypos (Q7–11 and Q12–15), and combination choices such as "B and C", "A, B and C", and "None of the above." Together they cover all 14 sections of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, from creating an agency through authority, ratification, estoppel, tort liability, and agents' duties, to partnership formation, property, management, partners' duties, dissociation and dissolution under the UPA and RUPA, Kovacik loss sharing, buyouts, LPs, LLPs, and LLCs. Every choice has its own explanation. Study mode checks each answer as you pick it; Exam mode lets you answer a full exam and then grade it.
