Con Law II · Chapters 1–6

# First Amendment Mock Exam

A full-length practice exam: sixty bar-style questions covering everything in the Chapters 1–6 outline, from Barnette and the Religion Clauses through incitement, prior restraints, true threats, and immoral speech, including every stretch exercise. Every question has new facts. The exam comes in four 15-question parts, each with a 30-minute clock and a question on every major topic, so you can take one part or all four. Nothing is scored until you submit. Then you get your results by topic, a list of rules to review, and an explanation for every answer choice.
