# Evidence Mock Midterm II

A second full-length practice exam: sixty new bar-style questions on everything tested in Classes 1–10, with no fact pattern repeated from the first mock midterm. They draw on the textbook problems, the rule chart, and Prof. Hornzell's slides and clips. The exam comes in four 15-question parts, each with the real midterm's 30-minute clock and a question on every major topic, so you can take one part or all four. Nothing is scored until you submit. Then you get your results by topic, a list of rules to review tonight, and an explanation for every answer choice.
