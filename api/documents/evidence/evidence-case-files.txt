# Evidence Case Files

Bar-depth application questions for Weeks 1–5. Each stem sets two or three rules against each other, so the answer turns on one fact: who the witness is, what is actually disputed, whose statement it was, or who opened the door. After you answer, every option shows its own rationale. Variations change one fact in an earlier question and ask whether the ruling changes.
