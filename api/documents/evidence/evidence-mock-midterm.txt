# Evidence Mock Midterm

Sixty new bar-style questions on everything tested in Classes 1–10: the readings, the rule chart, and Prof. Hornzell's slides, problems, and clips. The exam comes in four 15-question parts, each with the real midterm's 30-minute clock and a question on every major topic, so you can take one part or all four. Nothing is scored until you submit. Then you get your results by topic, a list of rules to review tonight, and an explanation for every answer choice.
