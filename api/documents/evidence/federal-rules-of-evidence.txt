# Federal Rules of Evidence

The full text of all 69 rules, as amended to December 1, 2024, with the Rule 801(d)(1)(A) amendment taking effect December 1, 2026 and the other recent amendments noted on their rules. Standard lines under a subdivision name the burden or balancing test it applies. Search by word, by "exact phrase" in quotes, or by cite (type 803(2) to go straight to the excited-utterance exception), filter by the course topic a rule belongs to, and follow cross-references between rules. Each rule shows how often the Evidence outline and the practice sets cite it.

Clear

Source: Federal Rules of Evidence, as amended to December 1, 2024 (House Committee on the Judiciary print, 2025). The rule text is the official text. The Advisory Committee Notes are not reproduced. "Standard" lines are study annotations that name the legal standard a rule applies, and they are not part of the rule. The notes on Rules 106, 107, 613, 615, 702, 801, 804, and 1006 summarize the Committee Notes on the 2023, 2024, and 2026 amendments, and the 2026 text comes from the Supreme Court's order of April 8, 2026. Subdivision headings are shown in title case where the print uses small capitals. Amendment years list the three most recent amendments.
