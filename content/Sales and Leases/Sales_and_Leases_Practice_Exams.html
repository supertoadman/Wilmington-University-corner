Sales and Leases Practice Exams

19 practice exams (285 questions) for the Week 10 Sales and Leases assessment, written in the style of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 stand-alone questions with five choices each, mixing rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…") with short hypos that ask who wins and why. Some choices are "None of the above" or "All of the above", and sometimes that is the answer, so those choices stay in exam order. Each exam samples the whole Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, from which law governs through excuse, and together they cover every section, case, and worked problem. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam (the real one is 30 minutes) and then grade it.
