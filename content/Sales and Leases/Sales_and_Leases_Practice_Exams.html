Sales and Leases Practice Exams

58 cumulative practice exams (870 questions) for the final exam, covering all of Weeks 1–14 and every section of the course outline, from which law governs and the battle of the forms through warranties, risk of loss, buyer's and seller's remedies, privity, and leases under Article 2A. Each exam follows Professor Richardson's sample-exam format: 15 stand-alone, five-choice questions that mix rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…") with short hypos asking who wins and why, plus "None of the above" and "All of the above" choices that are sometimes the answer. Every exam samples the whole course, and 334 questions come from the remedies and lease units in Weeks 8–14. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it. Pick a topic to drill one section; each topic shows its week and outline section.
