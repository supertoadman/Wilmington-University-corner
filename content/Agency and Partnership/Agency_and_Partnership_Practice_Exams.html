Agency & Partnership Practice Exams

14 practice exams (210 questions) for the Week 10 Agency & Partnership exam, written in the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 questions with five choices each. Questions 1–6 stand alone. Questions 7–11 and 12–15 share a hypo. Some choices combine others ("B and C", "A, B and C", "None of the above"), so the choices stay in exam order. Together the exams cover every section of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, and every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it.
