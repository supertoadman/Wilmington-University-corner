Agency & Partnership Practice Exams II

A second set of 14 practice exams (210 new questions) for the Week 10 Agency & Partnership exam, in the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 questions with five choices each. Questions 1–6 stand alone. Questions 7–11 and 12–15 share a hypo. Some choices combine others ("B and C", "A, B and C", "None of the above"), so the choices stay in exam order. Every hypo is new, none repeats a fact pattern from the first set, and together the exams again cover every section of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it.
