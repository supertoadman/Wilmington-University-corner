Civil Procedure Bar Questions

Bar-style multiple-choice questions covering Weeks 1–9 of Civil Procedure (Sparks, Fall 2026), Classes 1–16: every rule, case, and tip in the condensed course outline, from the court systems and sources of procedural law through personal jurisdiction, notice and service, federal question, diversity, supplemental and removal jurisdiction, venue, transfer and forum non conveniens, the Erie doctrine, pleading and Rule 11, and responding to the complaint. Each question sets out new facts and asks you to apply the rule, and every answer choice has its own explanation. Variations change one fact in an earlier question to test whether the result changes, and the last section mixes issues the way an exam does. ◆ marks a rule from outside the class materials, as in the outline.
