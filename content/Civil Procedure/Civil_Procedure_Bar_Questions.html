Civil Procedure Bar Questions

255 bar-style multiple-choice questions on Weeks 1–9 of Civil Procedure (Sparks, Fall 2026), Classes 1–16, from the court systems through personal jurisdiction, notice and service, subject-matter jurisdiction, removal, venue, the Erie doctrine, pleading, and responding to the complaint. Each question gives a new fact pattern and asks what a court should do, and the four choices are written to the same length and form, so the right answer can't be spotted by its wording. The wrong choices are the usual traps: the right result for the wrong reason, a correct rule that doesn't apply, and a misstatement of the rule. Every choice has its own explanation. The 32 variations change one fact in an earlier question to test whether the result changes, and the last section mixes issues the way an exam does.
