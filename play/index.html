How to play

Run
You run on your own. ← → change lanes, and at a corner they turn. ↑ or Space jumps, ↓ slides. On a phone, swipe, or tap to jump.
Gates
Each gate stops you for a question. There's no clock: take as long as you need. Use the keys 1–4 or tap an answer.
Corruption
A wrong answer spreads corruption. At half, the sanctum falls: the world turns, your philosopher grows horns, and the pursuer becomes a horned shade that runs closer and faster. Right answers drive it back, and a streak drives it back harder. If corruption overflows, the Fallen take a heart.
Review
When you miss, the gate shows the explanation and a link to the part of the course outline that covers it. Missed questions are saved, so you can run just those later.
Pickups
Laurels are points, worth double in the fallen realm. A wisdom scroll gives Insight, which strikes out a wrong answer at a gate. The aegis blocks one hit.
Hearts
Obstacles, falls and overflowing corruption each cost a heart. Lose all three and the Fallen catch you, but you can rise again and keep your place.
