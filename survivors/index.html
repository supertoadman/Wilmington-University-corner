        How to play
        Move
        WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone or with a mouse, press anywhere and drag. Esc pauses.
        Fight
        Your weapons aim and fire on their own. Destroyed foes drop soul gems; gather them to fill the bar at the top.
        Level up
        Each level stops the world for one question, with no time limit. Answer with a click or the keys 1–6 or A–F; cross out choices with ×.
        Boons
        A right answer lets you choose one of three: a new weapon, a weapon level, or a relic. A maxed weapon with its matching relic evolves. Three right in a row while corrupted offers a Purify card.
        Corruption
        A wrong answer makes you choose one of three corruptions (slower feet, weaker blows, more foes…). They stack, and the crypt darkens as they do. The answer, its explanation and the outline section to review are shown either way.
        The crypt
        A boss rises every three minutes and drops a reliquary. Braziers and coffins hide gold, food and the Lodestar, which pulls in every gem. Survive to 15:00 to see the dawn.
