Agency & Partnership

Business Associations, Weeks 1–8 (Bainbridge, Park & Rodrigues, 6th ed.). Contracts I

Contracts I, Weeks 1–9 (Epstein, Markell & Ponoroff, Making and Doing Deals, 7th ed.). Covers whether there is an enforceable agreement, the objective theory of assent, offers and their termination, options and firm offers, methods of acceptance and silence, the mirror-image rule and UCC § 2-207, misunderstanding, indefiniteness and letters of intent, consideration, modification, promissory estoppel, the statute of frauds, duress, and misrepresentation.

Civil Procedure

Civil Procedure, Weeks 1–9 (Spencer, Civil Procedure: A Contemporary Approach, 7th ed., and the Federal Civil Rules Supplement). Covers the court systems and sources of procedural law, personal jurisdiction from Pennoyer through Ford, notice and service of process under Rule 4, federal question, diversity, supplemental and removal jurisdiction, venue, transfer and forum non conveniens, the Erie doctrine through Shady Grove, pleading standards from Conley to Iqbal, Rule 11, and responding to the complaint under Rules 8, 12, and 55.

    Practice QuestionsSales and Leases

    Sales and Leases Practice Exams

    19 practice exams (285 questions) for the Week 10 assessment, written in the style of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 stand-alone, five-choice questions each, mixing rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…") with short hypos that ask who wins and why, plus "None of the above" and "All of the above" choices that are sometimes the answer. Each exam samples the whole Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, and together they cover all 16 sections: which law governs, formation and § 2-207, the statute of frauds, good faith and modification, express and implied warranties, title, quantity and price, delivery and acceptance, risk of loss, disclaimers, remedies, the parol evidence rule, assignment, termination, insecurity and repudiation, and excuse. Every choice has its own explanation. Study mode checks each answer as you pick it; Exam mode lets you answer a full exam and then grade it. Filter by exam or outline section, shuffle the questions, and retry the ones you missed.

    Practice QuestionsAgency & Partnership

    Agency & Partnership Practice Exams

    14 practice exams (210 questions) for the Week 10 exam, written in the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam: 15 five-choice questions each, with six stand-alone questions followed by two shared hypos (Q7–11 and Q12–15), and combination choices such as "B and C", "A, B and C", and "None of the above." Together they cover all 14 sections of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline, from creating an agency through authority, ratification, estoppel, tort liability, and agents' duties, to partnership formation, property, management, partners' duties, dissociation and dissolution under the UPA and RUPA, Kovacik loss sharing, buyouts, LPs, LLPs, and LLCs. Every choice has its own explanation. Study mode checks each answer as you pick it; Exam mode lets you answer a full exam and then grade it. Filter by exam or outline section, shuffle the questions, and retry the ones you missed.
