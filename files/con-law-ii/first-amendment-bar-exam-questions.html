Con Law II

First Amendment Bar Exam Questions

120 bar-style questions on all of Weeks 1–15 (Chapters 1–13), in four practice finals of 30, each sized like the final's 60-minute, 30-question multiple-choice part. Each practice final runs through the whole outline in order, and together they test every case and doctrine in it: Barnette, Kennedy, and Mahmoud; Smith and the ways a law fails neutrality, Carson and Establishment Clause funding, and RFRA; compelled speech, content-based restrictions, the unprotected categories, overbreadth, and vagueness; incitement, fighting words, hostile audiences, R.A.V., and prior restraints; true threats and speech integral to crime; obscenity, secondary effects, child pornography, age verification, and violent imagery; fraud, defamation, IIED, true information, and the right of publicity; Central Hudson and Zauderer; content and viewpoint discrimination, time, place, and manner rules, and expressive conduct; government speech and coercion; funding conditions and forum doctrine; employee and student speech; and social media and elections. Each question gives new facts and asks for the result, and the wrong choices give the right result for the wrong reason, apply the neighboring doctrine, or use the dissent's or a superseded view. Five choices each, and every choice is explained.
