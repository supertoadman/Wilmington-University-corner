About the sources & answer checks

Questions are transcribed from the three dedicated quiz PowerPoints and the Week 3 multiple-choice PDF in your Professional Responsibility folder. Split slides are joined, whitespace is cleaned, and missing A–D labels are restored in their original order. The wording of the questions and choices is retained, including source defects identified in the notes. General class discussion problems and the existing generated question banks are not part of this set.

  • Chapter 5 Multiple Choice.pptx: 8 questions.
  • PR Chapter 6 Multiple Choice.pptx: 10 questions.
  • PR Week 3 Multiple Choice.pdf: 11 Chapter 8A questions and 4 Chapter 8B–C questions.
  • Chapter 20 and 21 MC Questions.pptx: 11 Chapter 20 questions and 10 Chapter 21 questions.

Answers were compared with Chapter5_MC_Answers.docx, Chapter6_MC_Answers.docx, Chapter_20_21_MC_Answers.docx, PR_Ch8_Answer_Key.pdf, and PR_Ch8_Confidentiality_Answer_Key.pdf. Their authorship is not assumed. Explanations draw on Ethical Lawyering (2nd ed.), the Week 3–5 class slides, and the ABA rules and comments supplied in the folder. Each explanation identifies the relevant materials. Slide and PDF page numbers count from the beginning of the source file; a printed textbook page is labeled explicitly.

Three review-only items: Chapter 8A–5 has conflicting source answers; Chapters 20–8 and 20–9 were marked skipped in the quiz slides. These do not affect your score. The two skipped items are optional in quiz mode and always included in the answer view. Other material qualifications are displayed with the relevant answer.

The course-key answer for 8A–8 is preserved with its compelled-disclosure qualification. Supplemental checks of the ABA's Rule 1.6 comments, Rule 1.9 comments, and 28 U.S.C. § 2678 clarify the flagged items. This is a study aid for these course questions, not a substitute for a particular jurisdiction's rules.

All questions and explanations are embedded in this file. No sign-in, network connection, or external script is needed.
