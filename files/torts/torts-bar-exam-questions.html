Torts

Torts Bar Exam Questions

90 bar-style questions on all of Weeks 1–15, in three practice finals of 30, each sized like the final's 60-minute multiple-choice part. Each practice final runs through the whole course in syllabus order, and together they test every section of the outline: intent, battery, assault, false imprisonment, IIED, trespass to land and chattels, and conversion; consent, self-defense, defense of others and of property, recapture, the shopkeeper's privilege, and necessity; the Hand formula, the reasonable person, professionals and informed consent, negligence per se, notice, and res ipsa loquitur; cause in fact, lost chance, alternative and market share liability, proximate and superseding cause; duty and the failure to act; comparative fault, assumption of risk, the firefighter's rule, limitations and repose, and immunities; premises liability; compensatory and punitive damages; joint and several liability, release, settlement, contribution, and indemnity; vicarious liability; strict liability; and nuisance. Each question gives a fact pattern and asks for the result, and the wrong choices give the right result for the wrong reason, apply the neighboring rule, or use the minority or superseded view. Five choices each, and every choice is explained.
