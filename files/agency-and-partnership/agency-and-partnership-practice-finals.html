Agency & Partnership Practice Finals

4 practice finals (120 questions), each 30 five-choice questions sized like the final's 60-minute multiple-choice part and running through the Weeks 1–14 outline in order: creating an agency, authority, ratification and estoppel, tort liability, agents' duties, partnership formation, property, management and duties, grabbing and leaving, dissolution under the UPA and RUPA, Kovacik, buyouts, LPs and LLPs, and the LLC weeks (formation, the operating agreement, veil piercing, fiduciary duties, capital and expulsion, and dissolution). Professor Richardson's format: stand-alone questions mixing rule questions ("Which of the following is NOT…") with short who-wins hypos, plus "None of the above," "All of the above" and combination choices. Every choice is explained. Use Exam mode to take a final under time and grade it, or Study mode to check each answer.
