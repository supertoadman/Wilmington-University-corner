Agency & Partnership Concept Exams

14 concept exams (210 questions) for the Week 10 Agency & Partnership exam. They use the format of Professor Richardson's sample exam, with 15 questions and five choices each, but they test the rules themselves rather than long fact patterns. The questions turn on fine distinctions: the UPA (1914) against the RUPA, general partnerships against LLPs, LPs, LLLPs, and LLCs, the R2d against the R3d, and the defaults a partnership agreement can and cannot change. Questions 1–6 stand alone. Questions 7–11 and 12–15 share a focus note that names the topic and the statute to apply. Some choices combine others ("B and C", "A, B and C", "None of the above"), so the choices stay in exam order. Statute sections follow the 2026 supplement. Together the exams cover every section of the Weeks 1–8 condensed outline. Every choice has its own explanation. Use Study mode to check each answer as you go, or Exam mode to answer a full exam and then grade it.
