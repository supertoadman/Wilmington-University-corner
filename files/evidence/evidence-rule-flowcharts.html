Big picture

Start here. Evidence must be relevant (401), then survive any rule that excludes it (402), then the 403 balance. The four exclusionary families are the rest of the course; blue dashed arrows show where one rule hands off to another.

606(b) Jury secrecy

Why we screen evidence at all: once the jury has deliberated, its verdict is almost never reopened. Walk down the exceptions in order.

401-403 Relevance

The gate every item passes through: probative + material, conditional relevance, then the 403 safety valve and limiting instructions.

407-411 Policy rules

Five rules that bar socially useful conduct for one specific use. Read each column down. 410 is the outlier.

Character: the box

The forbidden inference (trait -> conduct on this occasion) and the genuine exceptions that pass THROUGH it, plus how character may be proved.

404(b) Other acts

Routes AROUND the box: other acts offered for a non-character purpose, and the three steps they must still clear.

Impeachment
