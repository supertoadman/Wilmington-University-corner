Evidence Best Answer Questions

69 tricky bar-style questions on all of Weeks 1–15, in two sets (34 and 35), each covering every topic. Each question has two or three defensible choices, and you have to pick the best one, the way the bar exam does. Often that is the answer you'd rather not pick: the evidence comes in for a narrow purpose, a protective rule doesn't apply, or a more precise reason beats a true but broader one. Together the sets reach every section of the outline, from jury secrecy and Rule 601 competency through the Confrontation Clause, Rule 703 bases, the best evidence rule, and the familial privileges. Five choices each, and every explanation says why the runner-up loses.
